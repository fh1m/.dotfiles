Sensei Signal is the existing laptop pink variant of GNOME Adwaita cursors, now deployed to Homie with its desktop configuration. Original artwork: GNOME Project, https://www.gnome.org/ . Source: https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/adwaita-icon-theme . This derived cursor artwork is distributed under CC-BY-SA 3.0; see COPYING. The original theme supplies all cursor states and aliases; the colour variant follows Sensei’s hot-pink design.
