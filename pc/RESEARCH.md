# Desktop research and decisions

Research date: 2026-10-13. Applied to Ryzen 5 3600X, GTX 1660 SUPER (TU116, 6 GiB), 16 GiB RAM and a directly attached LG 1080p display. Community examples inform appearance; upstream documentation and local acceptance tests determine technical settings.

Twenty-four focused searches covered these subjects in eight groups: NVIDIA Hyprland/GSP and CachyOS architecture; Reddit glass/pink Quickshell desktops and Hacker News desktop ergonomics; Alacritty/fontconfig/Qt typography; NVIDIA codec support and Chromium VA-API; mpv/NVDEC, wf-recorder/NVENC and animations; container CDI, PyTorch CUDA compatibility and ROS; Btrfs/Snapper and WirePlumber; Starship, fonts and Hacker News workflows. Additional searches verified current upstream releases. These searches are research coverage, not evidence that every candidate setting is suitable.

| Subject | Source | Desktop decision and observed result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NVIDIA Wayland | [Hyprland NVIDIA](https://wiki.hypr.land/Nvidia/), [NVIDIA modules](https://docs.nvidia.com/datacenter/tesla/driver-installation-guide/kernel-modules.html) | Matching packaged open modules, modeset/fbdev enabled; retain GSP. OpenGL and Vulkan identify the real NVIDIA GPU. |
| CPU tuning | [CachyOS tweaks](https://wiki.cachyos.org/configuration/general_system_tweaks/), [Linux performance methods](https://www.brendangregg.com/linuxperf.html) | Use supported v3 packages and schedutil; measure workloads before changing policy. No overclock or mitigation removal. |

AV1 hardware support is not assumed for Turing. |
