# Guest research — 20–21 October 2026

**110 targeted search queries across the ten requested people, in 37 batched
search calls**, followed by primary-page reading and local source checks. This
is a query count, not a claim that 110 different sources were read in full.
Additional follow-up lookups are not needed to reach that count. Raw search
responses and cached public texts stay in the private guest-research directory;
this public ledger records questions and decisions without republishing articles.

The shipped catalogue contains 51 source records. All 110 short excerpts match
inspected source text, with at most 25 quoted words per source in this catalogue.
Topic stories are editorial summaries, not automatically verified historical
claims. The 990 workshop variants are original prompts built from 110 curated
topics and nine explicit learning lenses; they are not 990 quotations or newly
discovered anecdotes. Count and provenance stay separate.

## Selection rules and rejected shortcuts

- Search snippets locate material; they are not sufficient proof of a quotation.
- Prefer author writing, code, published interviews and identified archives.
- Interviewer questions, book reviews, article headlines and another guest's
  words are not silently attributed to the featured person.
- Repositories are evidence of an implementation, not proof the named founder
  personally wrote every line. Organization docs are labelled as such when used.
- A failed HTTP download is recorded. Keller's primary article was inspected
  through the web reader, whose extract is retained privately, rather than
  pretending a direct download succeeded.
- Third-party automatic transcripts and quote aggregators are discovery leads,
  not automatic additions. No material from forum speculation becomes biography.
- Do not present Urban's metaphors or a builder's anecdote as learning-science
  proof. Do not present an old technical prediction as a current fact.
- Original interpretations, staged dialogue and workshop prompts have their
  own labels and speakers. Real-excerpt mode never selects a workshop variant.
