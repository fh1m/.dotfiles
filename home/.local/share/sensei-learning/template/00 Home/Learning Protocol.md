# Learn by confronting a model

1. Choose a capability, not a reading quota.
2. State a prediction before asking for an explanation.
3. Repair the smallest **gate**; learn useful **parallel** ideas through the build.
4. Run a test small enough to disagree with you. Record raw evidence and uncertainty.
5. Preserve failed predictions in Errors. Explain the correction causally.
6. Close the source: reconstruct the trace, equation, diagram or mechanism.
7. Perturb a boundary; transfer the idea to an unfamiliar situation.
8. Schedule a review when reconstruction exposes a gap.

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Seen the word |
| 1 | Recognize it |
| 2 | Repeat its definition |
| 3 | Solve a familiar example |
| 4 | Derive, explain or predict without the source |
| 5 | Build, break, debug and transfer |

Aim for 4 on important ideas, 5 on load-bearing mechanisms. Only you grade yourself.
A review is a reconstruction task, not a rereading appointment.
