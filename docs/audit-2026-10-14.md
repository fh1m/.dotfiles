### Research checked

- KDE Connect uses 1714–1764 on TCP and UDP. [KDE bug 349315](https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=349315) reports that a narrower range stops the phone connecting. [GSConnect wiki](https://github.com/GSConnect/gnome-shell-extension-gsconnect/wiki/Help) gives the same nftables rule.
- Syncthing: 22000/tcp, 21027/udp, and 22000/udp for QUIC ([Syncthing forum](https://forum.syncthing.net/t/ufw-rules-for-syncthing/15449)).
- Clipboard: `x-kde-passwordManagerHint` is a de facto convention set by KeePassXC and Firefox. Klipper has skipped such entries since 2018. Whether cliphist honours it is unconfirmed; the live `CLIPBOARD_STATE=sensitive` skip is the same convention in cliphist's form. Sources conflict on scope; see the search notes in the private file.
- hypridle: `before_sleep_cmd` fires on logind PrepareForSleep. The documented pattern is `before_sleep_cmd = loginctl lock-session`, with `lock_cmd` running on the resulting Lock signal, so one lock path serves both. `general:inhibit_sleep` decides whether suspend waits for the hook. [wiki.hypr.land hypridle](https://wiki.hypr.land/Hypr-Ecosystem/hypridle/).
- udev: `TAG+="uaccess"` is the mechanism for the logged-in desktop user, and `MODE="0666"` gives every account write access. [Arch forum](https://bbs.archlinux.org/viewtopic.php?id=306211) and the [Debian uaccess bug](https://lists.alioth.debian.org/pipermail/pkg-nvidia-devel/2014-May/010079.html) say the same. Community consensus, not official docs.
